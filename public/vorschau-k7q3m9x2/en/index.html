About me

I believe that we as human beings need real encounters in the real world. That is why, in 2011, I decided to make this my calling – and in 2012 I co-founded my first dance school. Today Hep Cat Club has two locations where people come together every day: in classes, at parties, at festivals and events. And with Tanz Atelier Erding, another dance school is now joining. Personally, I travel the world as a trainer, educator, consultant and speaker to help create more real places where communities can grow.
